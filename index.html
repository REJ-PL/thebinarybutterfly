About the book

Four lives bump into each other by chance at, more or less, the end of the world, and proceed to build their own consequences around themselves without ever quite meaning to.

It's a story about existence, money and power not always being the same thing, and people muddling through the toil of it all.

For readers of Adrian Tchaikovsky's Service Model and Emily Tesh's Some Desperate Glory.

Richard Peall

Richard Peall writes and designs games from Lincolnshire. After a career spent mostly in business intelligence, he took early retirement to make things of his own.

He publishes tabletop and video games as Afour games. The Binary Butterfly is his debut novel. It began life at the gaming table as a long-running cyberpunk campaign and refused to stay there.
